Privacy Policy for Better Half

Last updated: September 10, 2025

Noah Kirby ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile game "Better Half" (the "Game"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Game.
We Do Not Collect User Data

The Game is designed to be a completely private and secure experience. We want to be clear and transparent:

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information or non-personally identifiable information from you. This includes, but is not limited to, your name, email address, location, device information, or any data related to your in-game activity.

All game data and progress are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this information and it is not shared with any third parties.
Third-Party Services

Our Game does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.
Children's Privacy

Our Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. Given that we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.
Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
